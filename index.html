Your phone gives you a lost word of your own, a riddle for someone else's word, and a role. Nobody ever reads their own word. Each turn one person reads their riddle aloud. Everyone else picks the word on their own phone and locks it. Phones up! Every phone turns green (right) or red (wrong). Found if there is at most one red phone. The owner of the word stays silent: it is never for the one who lost it. One of you is the daemon. It wants words to stay lost. It is mortal: it has 4 lives, and every lost word burns one. When the candle is gone, it dies. More found than lost: we win. More lost: the daemon wins.
