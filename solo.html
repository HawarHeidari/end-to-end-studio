The Room of Lost Words

Everything lost turns up in one room,
never for the person who lost it.
1. Six people sit at a table: you and five guests. Each has lost a word.
2. In turn, someone reads a riddle about another person's word. Everyone picks the word and holds up their phone: green is right, red is wrong.
3. One red phone is forgiven. Two or more, and the word goes to the room of lost words.
4. One guest is the daemon. It answers wrong on purpose to keep words lost. It is mortal: every lost word burns its candle down. If more words are lost than found, it wins.
Solo version: the guests and the daemon are simulated in your browser. The real game is played with phones (party version).

The Room of Lost Words

Everything lost in a building turns up in one room, never for the person who lost it. Six people sit at a table. Each has lost a word, and each holds a riddle for somebody else's word. One of them is a daemon that wants the words to stay lost. It is mortal: every lost word burns its candle down, and everyone can see how much is left.

About this page: the real game is played around a table with 4 to 32 phones (party version). Here the five other guests and the daemon are simulated in your browser, so one person can play it.
